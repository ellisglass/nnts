# Xomsky: Short-Form Video Campaign Brief 🐹⚡

> **TikTok / Reels / YouTube Shorts Launch Campaign (9:16 Vertical)**  
> **Brand:** Xomsky (`com.almosteleven.xomsky`)  
> **Positioning:** Быстрый доступ к окнам конкретного профиля Chrome/Brave через Капс Лок + C/B + 1-4, быстрый и интуитивный доступ к выбранным приложениям через Капслок + Первая Буква, и копирование текста при выделении (Copy-on-Select).  
> **Slogan:** *"Tap the Hamster. Own the Flow."*  
> **Theoretical Foundation:** *Brain, Beauty, & Art: Foundations of Neuroaesthetics* (Oxford University Press, 2022)

## 1. The Core Creative Concept: The Cultural Subversion

Every developer has seen the absurd mobile "tap the hamster" coin clickers. We subvert this idle meme into an authentic developer superpower:

* **0.0–2.5s Cognitive Split Hook:**
  - *Expectation:* Someone frantically tapping a phone screen with an idle hamster coin game.
  - *Reversal:* Abrupt cut to a mechanical keyboard. A finger taps the physical **Caps Lock** key + First Letter of App — instant application jump.
* **Core Value Prop:** Stop wasting your taps on worthless virtual coins. Put Xomsky on your Mac: быстрый доступ к окнам конкретного Chrome/Brave профиля через Капс Лок + C/B + 1-4, мгновенный доступ к выбранным приложениям через Капслок + Первая Буква, и автоматическое копирование текста при выделении.

* **The 3 Authentic Creative Angles (Order of Priority):**
  1. *The Browser Profile Trap (Killer Feature #1):* macOS window management is broken for multi-profile workflows. Cmd+Tab doesn't separate profiles; Cmd+` cycles randomly. Without Xomsky, you feel crippled hunting for windows. With Xomsky: `Caps + C/B + 1..4` snaps straight to the exact profile window.
  2. *First-Letter App Reflex (#2):* Your hand is already on Caps Lock. Don't waste cognitive energy typing into Spotlight or Raycast search bars. `Caps + T` (Terminal), `Caps + I` (IDE), `Caps + A` (Agent), `Caps + N` (Notes).
  3. *Eliminating the Cmd+C Muscle Tax (#3):* If you selected text, 99.9% of the time you intended to copy it. Pressing Cmd+C is a redundant tax repeated hundreds of times a day. Xomsky copies on select automatically.

## 2. Neuroaesthetic Directives (From the Brandbook)

* **Law 1: Facial Reward & mOFC Activation (*Kindchenschema*):**
  - Xomsky's golden puffed cheeks occupy >60% of facial width. Large glossy eyes with crisp white reflections trigger immediate fixation in the Fusiform Face Area.
* **Law 2: Sensorimotor Mirroring & Embodied Simulation:**
  - Paws physically cradling the glowing mechanical keycap prime the viewer's left hand to reach for the Caps Lock key.
* **Law 3: The Infovore Principle & Perceptual Fluency:**
  - Bold, stylized 3D volumes on a deep midnight navy ground (`#0B1120`). Figure-to-ground contrast exceeds 10:1.
* **Law 4: Implied Motion & V5 Dynamic Flow:**
  - The luminous upward chevron (`⇪`) acts as a vertical motion vector, pulling eye gaze directly into the mascot's joyful face.
* **Law 5: Architectural Affordances:**
  - Sub-16ms native window switching depicted with zero stutter, reflecting 1-frame response fidelity.
